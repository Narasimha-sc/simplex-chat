- **Closing "Migrate here"**: swipe the sheet down from its top (`ui swipe 201 80 201 800`). It cannot close while importing or after the import.
- **Alerts**: in-app alerts close with "OK".
- **The passphrase field** ("Current passphrase…") is a secure field: its value is not readable. To replace a typed passphrase, tap the field, `ui key delete 30`, then type.

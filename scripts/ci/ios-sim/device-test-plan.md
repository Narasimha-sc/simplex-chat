# Device test plan: channels (creation, relays, roles, signing, verification) and preset chat list filters

The app as the scripted smoke test leaves it: the profile "citester", without conversations. The cases run in the order of the tables and build on each other: one channel, "ci news", owned by citester, and two more profiles in this app, "cibob" and "cicarol", that join it by its link. Do not reorder them: cicarol must join only after cibob has become a moderator (R48.0), so that cibob learns about cicarol, and after signing is on (R50.0), for R51.0.

## Setup and how to do things

- The channel uses the app's preset chat relays, on the internet. Creating it, joining it and adding relays wait for them: use `ui wait "<text>" 120` there, and take a shot if a wait fails.
- The app has exactly three preset chat relays: "SimpleX Chat Relay 1", "SimpleX Chat Relay 2" and "SimpleX Chat Relay 3" (operator "SimpleX Chat"). A relay that was ever added to the channel can never be added to it again, even after it is removed. So R47.8 turns off relays 2 and 3 for new channels, the channel starts with relay 1 only, and S4 turns 2 and 3 back on for R47.14 and R47.15.
- **Sending a message:** in the chat, tap the message field at the bottom (find it in `ui tree`: the text view next to the send button; for the channel's owner its placeholder is "Broadcast", for others "Message"), `ui type "<text>"`, then tap the send button at the right end of the field. Check the message then shows in the chat.
- **Channel info:** tap the channel name at the top of the chat (`ui find "ci news"`, the element with the smallest y; tap it with `ui tap-xy`). Go back with the navigation back buttons; close a sheet by swiping it down.
- **Message menu:** `ui long-press <x> <y>` on the message's text (centre from `ui find`) opens its menu: the reaction emojis in a row at the top ("👍" first), then "Info", "Moderate", …. "Info" opens the message's details; close them by swiping down.
- **Signatures are read as text, never from the seal icons:** a message's "Info" shows "Signed" (your own message), "Signed & verified" (someone else's, checked) or "Signature missing".
- **Switching profile:** open the picker with your profile image (`Image "Account"`), then tap the other profile's name in the row at the top of the picker (`ui find <name>`, then `ui tap-xy`; the picker's elements are in the tree even while it is closed, so open it first). The chat list then shows that profile's chats.
- **Adding a profile:** picker → "Your chat profiles" → "Add profile" → tap the field "Enter your name…" (`ui tap-value "Enter your name…"`), type the name, check the field's value in the tree (the keyboard may capitalize it: then fix it), tap "Create profile". The new profile becomes the active one; close "Your chat profiles" by swiping it down.
- **Copying the channel link** (as citester): channel info → "Channel link" → "Share link" → in the share sheet tap "Copy" (`ui find Copy`; if it is not in the tree, `ui tap-xy` at its position in the screenshot) → close the sheet and go back to the chat list.
- **Joining by the copied link** (from a profile without conversations): "Compose" → "Scan / Paste link" → "Tap to paste link". If iOS asks to allow pasting, tap "Allow Paste". An alert names the channel with "Open channel": tap it; the channel opens with "Tap Join channel" and a "Join channel" bar at the bottom: tap the bar, and wait up to 120 s until the chat shows "you are subscriber".
- **Member info in the channel:** channel info → "Subscribers" (as owner or moderator) or "Owners & contributors" (as subscriber) → the member's row.
- **Changing a role** (as citester): member info → the "Change role" picker → the role → alert "Change role?" → "Change".
- **Network & servers:** picker → "Settings" → "Network & servers". Under "Preset servers" tap "SimpleX Chat"; its "Chat relays" section lists the three relays. Tap a relay: its "Chat relay" screen has the switch "Use for new channels". Go back with the navigation back button (labelled "SimpleX Chat servers"), which keeps the change; back on "Network & servers", tap "Save servers".

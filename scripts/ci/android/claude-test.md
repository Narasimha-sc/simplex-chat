# Test: channels (creation, relays, roles, signing, verification) and preset chat list filters in the SimpleX Chat Android app

You are testing the SimpleX Chat Android app, which runs in an Android emulator on this machine. Drive it only with the `ui` helper, through Bash, one command per call: `ui <command> [args]`. Anything else is refused; don't look for ways around that, and if something blocks the test, report it. The commands are listed at the end.

Your working directory is where your output goes. `ui shot <name>` saves `screens/NN-<name>.png` and prints the text on screen, one element per line after its centre `x,y` in screen pixels. Read the PNG to see the screen (1080×2400). The text comes from the app's accessibility tree, so it is spelled exactly, but it can list an element that another screen covers: when the text and the picture disagree, trust the picture. "Above" means a smaller y. `ui tree` also lists text fields and buttons that have no text.

## What is set up

- The app is already running, on its first screen, with a new, empty database: never run `ui start` or `ui stop`. Begin with `ui shot start` to see it.
- `ui type` types into the field that has the focus: tap a field before typing into it. `ui key BACK` is Android's back button.
- Besides the chats, the chat list shows the app's own cards as list items until they are closed: "New 1-time link / Paste link / Scan" (once there is a conversation), "Bottom bar / Top bar", and "Invest in SimpleX Chat!". They are part of the list, not something drawn over it: leave them open.
- The channel uses the app's preset chat relays, on the internet. Creating it, joining it and adding relays wait for them: wait up to 120 s there (`ui wait "<text>" 120`).
- The app has exactly three preset chat relays: "SimpleX Chat Relay 1", "SimpleX Chat Relay 2" and "SimpleX Chat Relay 3" (operator "SimpleX Chat"). A relay that was ever added to the channel can never be added to it again, even after it is removed. So R47.8 turns off relays 2 and 3 for new channels, the channel starts with relay 1 only, and S4 turns 2 and 3 back on for R47.14 and R47.15.
- The emulator's Pictures folder has `test-card.jpg`, a TV test card 1280×720 (colour bars, a large circle, "1080" at the top and "TVB" at the bottom), and `simplex-logo.png`.

## How to do things in the app

- **New chat** is "Start new chat", the round button in the bottom toolbar. **Your profile picture** is at the left end of the bottom toolbar ("profile image placeholder" in the tree, the one with the largest y): tapping it opens the profile picker, with your profiles' names in a row at its top, "Your chat profiles" and "Settings".
- **Send a message:** tap the message field at the bottom (its placeholder is "Broadcast" for the channel's owner, "Message" for others), `ui type "<text>"`, then tap "Send Message". Check the message then shows in the chat.
- **Channel info:** tap the channel name at the top of the chat. The subscriber count ("N subscribers") shows under that name. `ui key BACK` closes the info and other screens one at a time.
- **Message menu:** `ui long-press <x> <y>` on the message's text (centre from `ui find`) opens its menu: a row of reaction emojis at the top (the first is 👍), then "Reply", "Info", "Moderate", ….
- **Signatures are read as text, never from the seal icons:** a message's "Info" shows "Signed" (your own message), "Signed & verified" (someone else's, checked) or "Signature missing".
- **Switch profile:** open the profile picker and tap the other profile's name in the row at its top. The chat list then shows that profile's chats.
- **Add a profile:** profile picker → "Your chat profiles" → "Add profile". On "Create profile" the name field has the focus: `ui type` the name, check it in the tree, and tap the "Create profile" button (the lower of the two "Create profile" texts; the upper one is the title; use `ui find` and `ui click`). The new profile becomes the active one; `ui key BACK` until the chat list shows.
- **Copy the channel link** (as citester): channel info → "Channel link" → "Share link". Android's share sheet opens: tap its "Copy" action (find it with `ui text`; it may be an icon labelled "Copy" at the top of the sheet). `ui key BACK` until the chat shows.
- **Join by the copied link** (from a profile without chats): "Start new chat" → "Paste link / Scan" → "Tap to paste link". An alert names the channel with "Open channel": tap it. The channel opens with "Tap Join channel" and a "Join channel" bar at the bottom: tap the bar, and wait up to 120 s until the bottom of the chat shows "you are subscriber".
- **Member info in the channel:** channel info → "Subscribers" (as owner or moderator) or "Owners & contributors" (as subscriber) → tap the member's name.
- **Change a role** (as citester): member info → tap the "Change role" row → tap the role → "Change" in the alert "Change role?".
- **Network & servers:** profile picker → "Settings" → "Network & servers". Under "Preset servers" tap "SimpleX Chat"; scroll to its "Chat relays" section, which lists the three relays. Tap a relay: its "Chat relay" screen has the switch "Use for new channels" (tap the switch at the right end of the row; find it in `ui tree`). `ui key BACK` goes back from the relay (the change is kept) and from "SimpleX Chat"; on "Network & servers" tap "Save servers" before leaving it.

- `ui do click-text "Get started" -- click-text "Enter profile name" 30 -- type citester -- shot profile`
- `ui do click-text "Start new chat" -- click-text "Create public channel" 10 -- wait "Channel name" -- shot new-channel`

`ui do` stops at the first command that fails, so a failed chain tells you where it stopped. Take a shot at each check, named after its case (`ui shot R47-7-owner`).
